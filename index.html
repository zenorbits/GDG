About the chapter

Built by developers, for developers.

GDG Navi Mumbai is a volunteer-led community under Google Developer Groups. We run free monthly sessions where engineers, students, and founders across Panvel, Vashi, and Thane get together to learn and build.

No talk is a product pitch. Every session ships something — a working demo, an open-source repo, or a deployed project you can point to on your resume by the time you leave.

Whether you're writing your first Android app or scaling a production API, there's a room here for you.

What we cover

Four tracks, one community.

Sessions rotate across Google's core developer surfaces — pick the track that matches what you're building.

Android

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and shipping to the Play Store — from your first layout to production releases.

Web

Modern frontend tooling, performance, and the platform APIs that ship in Chrome first.

Cloud

Google Cloud fundamentals, Firebase, and deploying real backends without the guesswork.

AI & ML

Gemini API, on-device ML, and practical model integration for apps people actually use.
